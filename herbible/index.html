What Her Bible does

  • Morning and evening prayer — a short guided prayer each day, built around a verse, a reflection and a prayer.
  • The Bible, read and heard — the King James Version with audio, plus free modern and public-domain translations, in English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), German, French, Spanish and Portuguese.
  • Reading plans and a verse of the day — topical plans, daily verses and short Bible quizzes.
  • Prayer journal, notes and highlights — kept on your device.
  • Word study — long-press a word in the King James text to see its original Hebrew or Greek meaning.
  • Gentle reminders — optional prayer and reading notifications that you control.

Her Bible is free and supported by ads. An optional one-time purchase or subscription removes them.

Signing in with Google (optional)

Her Bible works fully without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks only for your basic profile — your name, email address and profile photo — and uses it to create your account and back up your prayers, notes and progress so they can be restored on a new device. We never see your Google password, and we never sell your data. The details are in our Privacy Policy.
