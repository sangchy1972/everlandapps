Delete Your Account & Data

Her Bible, published by Everland Technology Limited · Last updated: June 2, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Her Bible account and the data associated with it. Her Bible is published by Everland Technology Limited.

Most of your Her Bible data — your profile, prayers, notes, highlights, bookmarks, reading progress, mood check-ins and reminder settings — is stored only on your device. Signing out or uninstalling the app deletes that data from your device immediately. The steps below also cover any data associated with your account so you can have it fully removed.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Her Bible.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Sign out. This removes your account information and saved content stored on your device.
  4. To remove any remaining local data, uninstall the app from your device.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email us from the email address associated with your account:

  • To: admin@everlandapps.com
  • Subject: Delete my Her Bible account
  • Please include the email address associated with your account so we can verify the request.

We will verify and complete your request within 30 days.

What is deleted

Upon deletion we remove the personal data and content associated with your account, including:

  • Account information (your name and email address);
  • Your profile photo;
  • Your in-app content: prayers, notes, verse highlights, bookmarks, saved verses, Bible and reading-plan progress, mood check-ins, achievements, and reminder settings.

What may be kept, and for how long

We retain only what we are legally required to keep — for example, transaction and purchase records needed for tax and accounting purposes — for the period required by applicable law. Such records do not contain your devotional content. Everything else is deleted as described above.
